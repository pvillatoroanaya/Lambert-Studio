Or build your own

Compares the route families mission designers use for this trip (direct, Venus–Earth flybys, Jupiter flybys…), finds their launch windows and trip times, the rocket each one needs, and recommends one. Takes a few seconds.

Advanced: choose the flybys and dates yourself

Tries thousands of launch dates, flight times and loops around the Sun, keeps the physically possible routes, and refines the cheapest. The same planet can be used twice in a row. Routes with 3–4 flybys take up to ~30 s.
